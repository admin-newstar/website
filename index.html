What we acquire and build.

Four strategies, one thesis: durable cash flow in growing submarkets, with construction we control from the first drawing to the final certificate of occupancy.

New Commercial Construction

Ground-up retail, office, medical and light industrial developed on entitled and pre-entitled sites. We manage design, permitting, GC selection and delivery in house.

  • Build to suit
  • Pad sites
  • Shell & core

Multifamily

Garden, mid-rise and workforce housing in markets with real job and population growth. We underwrite to in-place fundamentals, not to a refinance that has to happen.

  • New development
  • Value add
  • Workforce housing

Mixed-Use

Residential over retail and live-work projects that give a neighborhood a center of gravity. Blended income streams reduce single-tenant and single-sector exposure.

  • Retail podium
  • Live-work
  • Infill

Absolute NNN

Single-tenant net lease assets with credit tenants, long primary terms and zero landlord responsibility. Truly passive ownership with contractual rent escalations.

  • Credit tenants
  • Zero landlord duties
  • Rent escalations

A process built to be repeated.

Every opportunity moves through the same four gates. If it does not clear one, it does not move to the next.

01

Source

Off-market and broker relationships in growth corridors, filtered by demographics, absorption and supply pipeline before a single site visit.

02

Underwrite

Conservative rent, exit cap and construction-cost assumptions. Stress tested for interest rate, lease-up and hard-cost movement.

03

Execute

Entitlement, design, GC bidding and construction management with weekly cost and schedule reporting against the original budget.

04

Own

Stabilize, lease and hold. Active asset management, proactive capital planning and transparent reporting to every partner.

Our acquisition criteria.

We move quickly on deals that fit and pass on the ones that don't. Brokers, developers and sellers know within days, not weeks.

  • Ground-up sites, entitled land and covered land plays
  • Stabilized Absolute NNN with 10+ years of primary term remaining
  • Multifamily and mixed-use in growth and secondary markets
  • Direct principal purchases, joint ventures and development partnerships
  • 1031 exchange timelines accommodated

HEADQUARTERS

Sheridan, Wyoming.

Wyoming's business climate — no corporate or personal income tax, strong entity protections and straightforward governance — lets us keep overhead low and put capital into the ground instead of into structure.

Long-term owners, not traders.

NewStar Ventures was formed to do one thing well: put capital into real assets that produce income for decades, and build them properly the first time.

We underwrite every deal as if we will hold it forever, because most of the time we will. That changes what we buy, how we build it, and which partners we work with.

Whether you're a broker with an off-market listing, an owner considering a sale, a developer seeking a capital partner, or an investor looking at net lease, we'd like to hear from you.
